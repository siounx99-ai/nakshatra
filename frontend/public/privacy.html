Privacy Policy — Nakshatra · எனது ஜோதிடர்

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

To calculate your Vedic Jathagam, Pancha Patchi, and Thirumana Porutham accurately, Nakshatra processes the birth details you enter: Name, Gender, Date of Birth, Time of Birth, and Birth Place (latitude/longitude).

We also generate an anonymous 8-digit privacy code so you can check marriage compatibility without sharing your personal birth time or date publicly.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Calculate sidereal planetary positions, Dasa/Bhukti, and daily Pancha Patchi timings.
  • Provide personalized answers in the "Ask Jothidar" feature using your chart context.
  • Store your chart securely in Google Firebase Firestore when you save or generate a code.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal or birth data to third parties.

3. Local Storage & Deletion

Your profile and language preferences are stored locally on your device. You can edit or clear your data anytime from your device settings or browser storage.

4. Disclaimer

Nakshatra provides traditional Tamil Vedic astrology and Pancha Patchi insights for cultural, educational, and personal guidance purposes only. It is not a substitute for medical, legal, or financial advice.

5. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, please visit nakshatra-plum.vercel.app.
